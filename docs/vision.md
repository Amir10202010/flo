# Velnox — Vision

Product: Velnox — AI Gmail inbox for client-facing teams.

Problem: Managers lose clients due to slow replies and lack of prioritization across Telegram and Gmail.

Solution: Unified inbox with AI prioritization, risk detection, and suggested actions.

Target user: Small-service managers (salons, clinics, tutors, agents).

Success metrics: reduce average response time, increase reply rate, surface HOT conversations.
